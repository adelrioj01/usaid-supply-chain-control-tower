# Dashboard blueprint

Use a 16:9 canvas at 1280 × 720. Place page navigation across the top and a compact filter rail on the left. Show metric coverage beside freight- and weight-based visuals.

## Page 1: Executive overview

- Cards: shipments, on-time rate, late shipments, commodity value, freight cost and freight coverage
- Monthly trend: shipment count and on-time rate
- Country map: shipment count sized by commodity value
- Bar chart: top countries by late shipments
- Table: late, high-value shipments with country, mode, vendor and delay days

## Page 2: Delivery performance

- On-time rate by country and year
- Delay distribution and average late days
- Matrix: vendor by destination country
- Shipment-mode comparison
- Drill-through table at shipment grain

## Page 3: Freight efficiency

- Freight cost, freight per kg and logistics cost rate
- Scatter: weight versus freight cost by shipment mode
- Freight cost by country and mode
- Distribution of freight per kg
- Coverage warning for missing freight and weight

## Page 4: Commodity portfolio

- Commodity value and units by product group
- First-line value share
- Top molecules or test types
- Brand and dosage-form mix
- Decomposition tree: product group, subclassification, molecule and brand

## Page 5: Vendor and site performance

- Vendor shipment count and on-time rate
- Manufacturing-site performance
- Risk matrix: commodity value versus late rate
- Detail table with average lead time and destination coverage

## Page 6: Country profile

- Drill-through country summary
- Yearly shipment and delivery trend
- Shipment-mode mix
- Top vendors and products
- Late shipment detail

## Page 7: Data quality

- Source and processed row counts
- Freight and weight coverage
- Missing values by field
- Shipment attribute conflicts
- Source checksum, download date and reporting period
